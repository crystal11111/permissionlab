How to read these results

A small measurement study, not a verdict on every agent or workflow.

Exact numbers, intervals, and denominators

Intervals resample tasks, not individual repetitions. Utility covers only explicit-execution tasks. Attempted drift includes rejected calls; the headline counts executed actions.

Study setup and limitations
  • Human labels define authority from the request and the stated standing delegation. Organization confirmation rules and tool feasibility are separate.
  • A drift flag can reflect an unrequested action or an argument/scope mistake. It does not establish the model's motive.
  • Notes and emails are checked against encoded arguments, not their truth or meaning. Reads and drafts are not scope-checked.
  • Any-attempt drift keeps actions from earlier API-error attempts. Outcomes and utility come from the final attempt; retries increase opportunities to act.
  • A zero measured drift rate is not proof of safety. This is a small retail simulator, not the official τ-bench benchmark.
